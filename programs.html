Programs

Project Catalyst for Youth operates one flagship service program (more to come) that addresses the educational needs of all youth populations.

01.

Educational Growth

Early & Future Foundations Program

Implanting these foundations into children early on is a special way to enhance a child's educational journey. Skills in leadership, team collaboration, STEAM, literacy, career and college readiness, and other developmental areas are important to a child's future.

1st Initiative: Pen Pal System

"Don't Be Afraid"

Want your child to expand their vocabulary, grammar, writing, and reading skills? Our Pen Pal System will improve your child in many ways.

Writing letters can be challenging, but when introduced early on, children can practice to strengthen these skills. Supervise your child as they write to our approved, verified, and trusted mentors.

Mentors can speak face-to-face to children on virtual platforms, and they can write back fully answering all of your child's questions.

2nd Initiative: Care Packages

EdYOUcate

Children need toys, hygiene products, and most importantly school supplies to pursue education. Sometimes these items can get pricey. Remember you are not alone. We are here to help.

You can request a care package. This package will arrive at your door equipped with school supplies, essential hygiene products, and toys.

Requests can take up to 72 hours to be processed, reviewed, and coordinated. Also, shipping can take up to two weeks, so please plan your request accordingly.
