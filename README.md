# Project Catalyst for Youth

Source code for the [Project Catalyst for Youth](https://projectcatalystforyouth.org/) website.

## About the Organization

Project Catalyst for Youth (PCY) is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN: 42-2740932) founded in 2026 by Sheena Patel. PCY works to improve access to education, resources, and support for children in underserved communities.

The organization is entirely youth-run — every volunteer, officer, and ambassador is between the ages of 10 and 25. Youth leading youth.


### What We Focus On

| Focus | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **STEAM Equity** | Underrepresented youth often lack access to critical technology due to regional poverty. |
| **Literacy** | Too many children grow up without foundational reading, writing, and grammar skills. |
| **Representation** | Every child deserves to see themselves reflected in their education. |

### Programs

**Early & Future Foundations Program** — building early skills in leadership, team collaboration, STEAM, literacy, and college and career readiness.

- **Pen Pal System ("Don't Be Afraid")** — children write letters to approved, verified mentors to strengthen vocabulary, grammar, writing, and reading.

- **Care Packages ("EdYOUcate")** — requestable packages of school supplies, hygiene products, and toys delivered to families in need.

