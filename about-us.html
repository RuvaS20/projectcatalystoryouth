Young people, together.

At Project Catalyst for Youth

We believe that youth have the right to education no matter their demographics.

We're a youth-led movement that challenges current educational issues, changing who leads, who serves, and how education for ALL children constitutes.

Our Why

Founded in 2026, Project Catalyst for Youth (PCY) was created to support youth education, taking a holistic approach and proving that young people have the power to drive real change.

Our What

We work to normalize the educational experience and foster a supportive community through creative, emotionally uplifting services. From virtual events to community resources, PCY delivers care in a way that only youth can.

Our How

We aspire to be a leading, global nonprofit pushing for a change in hundreds of communities not only ours. Youth leading youth to success powers ultimate impact and change.
